Privacy policy

Axo: ADHD Focus & Habits · Effective and last updated: 11 October 2026

Your thoughts are personal. This policy explains what Axo accesses, where information lives, and the choices you have.

The short version

The current Axo app stores your progress on your device. It has no Axo account, cloud sync, advertising SDK, or remote analytics service. Optional Android focus protection processes app-usage events locally. Nothing is posted automatically.

This policy applies to the mobile app Axo: ADHD Focus & Habits (Android package com.axo.focus) and this Axo website. The developer’s privacy and support contact is appcraft.dev1@gmail.com. “We” and “our” refer to the developer of Axo.

Information stored on your device

Axo uses local app storage to provide its features and remember your progress. Depending on what you choose to use, this includes:

  • Your companion’s name, onboarding choices, app preferences, and tutorial progress.
  • Step titles, task plans and actions, routines, completion status, and selected focus durations.
  • Focus-session dates, durations, pauses and progress, daily intentions, and reflections you write.
  • Earned pebbles, companion progress, owned decorations, and their habitat positions.
  • Reminder settings, permission-prompt status, widget/timer state, and optional focus-protection settings.
  • Local Plus-preview interaction counts and invitation timing. These are on-device records, not remote analytics or purchase data.

We do not operate a server that receives this app progress. The app does not request your contacts, messages, microphone, camera, precise location, or health records. Device time and timezone support local reminders and session dates. Where used, motion-sensor input animates the habitat on-device and is not sent to us or retained as a motion history.

Local app storage is protected by the operating system’s app-access controls. It is not a separate encrypted vault. Your device’s security, backup settings, and who can access your unlocked device also matter.

Optional Android focus protection

Focus protection is a feature you can choose to enable. Axo displays available launcher apps—their names, package identifiers, and icons—so you can select distractions. Your selected package identifiers are saved locally.

With your explicit consent and Android Usage Access permission, Axo reads recent app foreground/background events during an active protected focus session to detect whether a selected app is open. Android may provide events for other apps within the queried time window; Axo processes these locally to determine the foreground app. It does not upload them, build a retained browsing or app-usage history, or use them for advertising.

With permission to display over other apps, Axo shows a focus shield over a selected distracting app. Axo may request temporary audio focus to encourage that app to pause audio. This is best-effort, not an operating-system app lock, a guaranteed audio blocker, or parental control. It does not read the content inside other apps.

A visible foreground service supports active protection. Monitoring stops when the session ends, is paused, or required access is revoked. You can disable focus protection in Axo’s Preferences and revoke Usage Access or display-over-other-apps permission in Android settings. Core focus sessions work without these permissions. iOS Screen Time protection is not implemented in the current version.

Permissions and why they exist

Notifications
Optional local focus-completion, break, and daily reminder alerts, plus an active timer notification. Depending on your lock-screen settings, notification information may be visible to people nearby. You can manage this in Axo and device settings.
Exact alarms / precise scheduling
Optional Android access for timely timer alerts. Without it, Android may delay reminders. It does not provide access to your calendar.
Usage Access and display over other apps
Only for the optional Android focus-protection feature described above. These are not required to use the basic app.
Foreground services
Keep the live timer notification and optional focus shield working during a user-started session while Axo is in the background.
Restart after device boot
Restore eligible local reminders and companion state after a restart. This is not permission to collect unrelated device activity.
Internet and Google Play billing
The release declares internet and billing permissions and bundles Google Play Billing Library 8.3.0 to prepare for store products. The current preview does not initiate purchases or integrate RevenueCat. These permissions alone do not mean your reflections or progress are uploaded. We will update this policy and the relevant disclosures before enabling new data flows.

Sharing and third-party services

Achievement cards

If you choose to share, Axo creates an image containing the achievement shown in the preview, such as focused time and date. A step title is included only if you opt in. Reflections are not included. Axo passes the image to the native share sheet; you choose the destination and whether to send it. The receiving app or service handles the image under its own policies. We cannot delete copies you send to another service.

Device backups and platform services

Your operating system may back up or restore app data according to device settings and platform policies. This is separate from Axo cloud sync, which does not exist in the current app. Manage backups through your device or platform account. App stores and device providers may separately process installation, account, diagnostic, or other platform information under their own policies.

Support enquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and any information you voluntarily include. We use them to answer your request, investigate issues, and maintain necessary support records. Do not send passwords, payment details, private reflections, or sensitive health information. Our support mailbox uses Gmail, which is subject to Google’s privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information you send to support if required by applicable law, or where necessary to protect legal rights or respond to a security issue.

This website

The website contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, embedded social feeds, or sign-up form. Its interactive previews use temporary in-memory state; they do not write cookies or browser storage and are not saved or submitted to us. Fonts and artwork are served with the site rather than loaded from a third-party font service.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes technical request information, including visitors’ IP addresses, for hosting and security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. External links and email services have their own policies.

Retention and deletion

App progress remains in local storage until you delete applicable entries, clear the app’s storage through device settings, or remove the app. On Android, open the device’s app settings for Axo and use the storage/data-clear option to erase local app data. Names vary by device. Clearing only the cache does not erase progress. Uninstalling may not remove separately retained operating-system backups or images you exported.

Temporary share images are stored in Axo’s cache. When creating a new share image, Axo attempts to remove its own cached share images older than one day. The operating system may clear the cache earlier. Images saved or shared outside Axo remain under your control or the receiving service’s control.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and fulfil applicable legal or security obligations. Contact us to request deletion of information held in support records. We cannot remotely access or erase app progress that exists only on your device. To remove backed-up copies, use the relevant platform’s backup controls.

Your choices and privacy enquiries

You can edit or delete steps, opt out of reminders, turn off optional focus protection, revoke device permissions, choose what goes in a share card, and clear local app data. There is no Axo account to delete.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our processing of information you have sent us, and to complain to a relevant data-protection authority. Email appcraft.dev1@gmail.com with your request. We may need proportionate information to confirm that the request relates to you. These choices do not limit rights provided by applicable law.

Children’s privacy

Axo is not designed or marketed for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13 through support enquiries. If you believe a child has provided us with such information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Local app content is not transmitted to our servers.

Updates and contact

If the app’s features, payments, or data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Material changes will be communicated where required. This policy describes the current app, not unimplemented features.
