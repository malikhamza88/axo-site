Manage local data

Axo has no cloud account in the current version. Your progress is stored on your device. Delete individual steps in the app or use Android’s app storage/data-clear option to remove local progress.

Read deletion and backup details

Adjust focus permissions

Focus protection is optional. Turn it off in Axo’s Preferences, or revoke Usage Access and display-over-other-apps access in Android settings. Focus sessions still work without it.
