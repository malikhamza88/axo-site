# Axo website design decisions
A marketing site for Axo: ADHD Focus & Habits. Buildless HTML/CSS/JS for a standalone public GitHub Pages repository. No Flutter code or private release material belongs here.

## Compact design system
- Pearl: #F8FCFA — page surface
- Water: #E9F5F3 — quiet supporting sections
- Deep water: #173D49 — headings and primary text
- Blush: #FFC6D7 — invitations and interaction feedback
- Plum: #4A283D — ink on blush
- Pond: #22695B — secondary accents
- Nunito, self-hosted from the app with its OFL license. Heavy rounded headings, calm readable body.
- Layout: left-aligned editorial headline, a large organic habitat on the right; a sequential interactive feature preview, followed by practical capabilities, FAQ, and honest pre-release CTA.
- Principal memorable element: Axo inside its own illustrated habitat. Don't use fabricated testimonials or pretend product screenshots.
- Motion: brief introductory swim and user-triggered greeting; no perpetual decorative animation. Reduced motion disables these.
- Legal pages: static HTML, readable without JavaScript, linked contents, date, contact, optional print.

## Review before building
The skill search's Swiss/orange palette does not fit Axo. The narrower wellness recommendation also doesn't fit the established app identity. Keep verified accessibility/performance guidance; use Axo's existing mint, blush, Nunito and habitat art instead of importing a generic SaaS design system. The demo is explicitly illustrative, not a real focus session. No fake store availability, pricing, claims of clinical benefit, or social proof.

Concept:
    brand                              navigation
    big, friendly headline             living habitat illustration
    explanation + next action          tap to greet / change lighting
    quiet factual values strip
    first step / focus / reward         interactive illustrative demo
    practical capabilities
    kindness narrative + FAQ
    Android testing enquiry
    privacy / terms / support
